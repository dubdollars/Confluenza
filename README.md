# Confluenza

A living record of where the AI/macro investment thesis stands — distilled from primary-source
interview transcripts, weighted by cross-source **confluence**, and stress-tested for bias.

The whole app is a single self-contained `index.html` file (no build step, no dependencies).
Open it in any browser, or view the live version via GitHub Pages.

## Views
- **Velocity Board** — themes ranked by momentum (how fast conviction is moving), not just level.
- **Themes** — deep dives with conviction scores, independent-voice counts, and dated changelogs.
- **Timeline & Predictions** — catalyst roadmap plus a hit/miss prediction scorecard.
- **Watchlist** — tickers mapped to themes with eyes-wide-open risk labels.
- **Source X-Ray** — bias check: what voices are in the library and which are missing.
- **Methodology** — how scoring, velocity, chronology and bias control work.

## Disclaimer
Research prototype, not investment advice. Content reflects the (biased, self-interested) views
of the transcript speakers. Tickers are research candidates only; prices/valuations are not live.
Do your own diligence.
